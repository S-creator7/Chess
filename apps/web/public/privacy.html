Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Chess Live (web and Android) collects account email, display name, a password hash, game moves, clocks, ratings, and in-game chat so we can run rated matches.

We do not sell your data. Opponents see your display name, rating, moves, and chat — not your email.

You can delete your account in the Android app or on the Account page of the web portal. Login is then disabled and your public name becomes “Deleted player”.
